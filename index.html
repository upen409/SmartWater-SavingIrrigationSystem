🌱 Smart Water-Saving Irrigation System

A simple educational website that uses soil-moisture readings to recommend when a plant may need water.

Prototype note: This is a software simulation. The moisture value is entered by the user; no physical sensor or pump is connected. The threshold is an example for demonstration, not a rule for every plant or soil.

1. Enter soil moisture

Move the slider to simulate a reading from 0% (very dry) to 100% (very wet).

35%

Threshold: 35%

2. Recommendation

Check the reading

Choose a moisture value and press “Check watering recommendation”.

How the decision works

  • If moisture is below the threshold, recommend checking whether watering is needed.
  • If moisture is at or above the threshold, recommend waiting and checking again later.
  • Always consider plant type, soil, weather, and the actual condition of the plant.

This website gives a recommendation only. It does not automatically water the plant.

4. Project learning

  • Problem: Unnecessary watering may waste water.
  • Users: Home gardeners, school gardeners, and small-plant growers.
  • SDG connection: SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation; responsible water use supports water sustainability.
  • Future upgrade: Connect a real moisture sensor and microcontroller, after safe adult supervision and testing.
